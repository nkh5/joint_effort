## Using it on the arm

The zero is a property of the motor (output shaft vs housing), so it carries over to the arm as long as:
the horn is not taken off its spline, no offset is rewritten (sts_set_center / one-key calibration),
and each motor goes back to the same joint. What the bench cannot know, per joint: (1) which multiple of
90 deg separates the bench 0 from the link direction, (2) the sign once mounted, (3) the URDF angle of that
pose (the CAD frames are not aligned to motor sides - the old datums were -0.416, -0.841, 1.547 ...).
One reference pose per joint on the rebuilt arm settles all three.
